Questions

How do I activate my key?

Right after checkout, click "Open in Burnfeed" and the app activates itself. Your key is also in the receipt email from Dodo Payments: paste it into the License tab of the Burnfeed app.

How does Burnfeed Team work?

You get one key. Share it with up to four other people; each of you activates it on up to two Macs, ten Macs in all. Anyone can free their Mac from the License tab to hand the seat to someone else.

I got a new Mac.

On the old Mac, open the License tab of the Burnfeed app and choose "Deactivate this Mac", then enter your key on the new one. If the old Mac is gone, email us and we'll free the seat.
