Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Burnfeed is made by Priyam Ventures Private Limited. In short: Burnfeed does not collect your data, track you, or show ads.

What stays on your Mac

Burnfeed runs entirely inside Safari on your Mac. Your settings, your quotes and the pages you visit are never sent to us or anyone else. To hide feeds, Burnfeed needs access to the sites it works on (Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and Reddit); it uses that access only to change how those pages look on your screen.

Rule updates

Sites change their layouts, so Burnfeed checks burnfeed.app a few times a day for updated hiding rules. This is an ordinary file download with no identifiers, settings or browsing data attached. Like any request on the web, it reaches our host (Cloudflare) with your IP address and browser version, which are kept only in standard server logs and are not used to identify or track you.

Reports and email

If Burnfeed notices it can't find a site's feed, it can prepare an email report for you. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it from your own email app; it contains the site, the page path, the rules version, your Burnfeed and browser version, and anything you add. If you email us for support, we keep the email only to reply and never use it for marketing.

This website

burnfeed.app uses no analytics, cookies or trackers.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any information. We would disclose an email you sent us only if you asked us to or the law required it.
