What it does. Briefly.

  • Six sites, your call. Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and Reddit. Switch each site, or parts of one, on or off.
  • A quote instead. Where the feed was. Use the built-in quotes or add your own, and pick how a burned feed looks.
  • Breaks that end. Show the feed for 5 minutes, or pause everything for 15 minutes or an hour. Then it burns again on its own.
  • Private. Your browsing never leaves your Mac. Burnfeed collects nothing. Privacy policy
